VP stuck hai? Ye 4 signs check karo.

Agar aap inme se kisi problem ka samna kar rahe hain, toh aap akele nahi hain:

01

Offline leads aati hain, close nahi hoti.

Aap time, travel aur energy lagate ho — nutrition club, ghar-ghar visit — lekin lead cold hi rehti hai. VP wahin ki wahin.

02

Business referral ke bharose chal raha hai.

Referral achha hai, par predictable nahi. Iss month kitni VP aayegi, ye referral decide karta hai. Ye system nahi hai.

03

Instagram par bas random content.

Dance video, Zoom recording, ya bina hook ki reel — aur ummeed ki DM aa jayega. Content post ho raha hai, leads nahi ban rahi.

04

Ads pehle chalaye, result nahi aaya.

Sirf "Boost Post" karna strategy nahi hoti. Galat targeting, weak offer, aur follow-up hi nahi — phir blame Meta ads ko mil gaya.

Agar inme se 2 bhi tick hue, toh problem aap mein nahi hai. Problem system ke bina marketing karne mein hai.
