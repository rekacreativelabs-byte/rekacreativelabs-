KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything founders ask us about Meta ads agency management, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and growth engines.

What does a dedicated Meta Ads Agency do?

A specialized Meta ads agency manages your entire advertising infrastructure across Facebook and Instagram. At Reka Creative Labs, we go beyond media buying: we engineer high-converting video and static creatives, configure Meta Conversions API (CAPI) for lossless attribution, conduct multivariate audience testing, and optimize the post-click conversion funnel to drive down your Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

What is Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and how do we calculate it?

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is calculated with the formula: CAC = (Total Marketing Spend + Total Sales Expenses) ÷ New Customers Acquired. It is the single most critical unit economics metric for founders. You can use our interactive Free CAC Calculator to measure your exact payback period and LTV:CAC ratio.

How does AI marketing accelerate growth compared to traditional marketing?

Traditional agencies take 3–4 weeks just to draft ad copies and deploy pages. We leverage generative AI workflows for rapid ad creative iteration, automated intent-based lead nurturing via WhatsApp bots, and programmatic SEO. This compresses 30-day testing cycles down to 48 hours, allowing you to find winning ad creatives at a fraction of the cost.

What average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) can we achieve?

Our clients consistently achieve between 3x and 10x verified ROAS depending on their market tier, average order value (AOV), and customer lifetime value. We optimize ad spend continuously by eliminating fatigued ad sets and reallocating capital into top-performing creative winners.
